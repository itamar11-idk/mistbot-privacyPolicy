Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 3, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to MistBot's Discord bot features only.

MistBot is a community tool for Attack on Titan: Revolution players. This policy explains what data MistBot processes through Discord, how it is used, when it is shared, and how you can request access, correction, or deletion.

2. Data We Process

2.1 Data You Provide Through Commands or Messages

  • Vouch data: vouch submissions, carrier identity, vouch type, totals, and related metadata.
  • Marketplace data: listing text (giving/looking for), optional notes, your Discord user ID, and listing status.
  • Safety data: scam reports, blacklist appeals, descriptions, and evidence attachments you submit.
  • Lobby data: lobby settings, participant lists, host information, and temporary private server details if you post them.
  • AI prompts: questions you send to MistBot AI features.

2.2 Data From Discord APIs or Bot Events

  • Account identifiers: Discord user ID, username/display name, and avatar URL.
  • Server context: guild/server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and configuration needed for per-server behavior.
  • Message content in configured trade channels: Mist-Connect scans trade-channel messages to detect trade intent.
  • Operational metadata: timestamps, command usage, error/health telemetry, and anti-abuse/rate-limit data.

2.3 Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • Passwords or external login credentials.
  • Payment card or banking data.
  • Message content outside bot-relevant contexts, except where required by Discord interactions or explicitly configured channel monitoring.

3. Why We Use Data

We process data to:

  • Operate bot commands and features.
  • Track vouches, profiles, and leaderboards.
  • Publish and manage marketplace listings.
  • Detect likely trade posts in configured trade channels.
  • Run scam-reporting, blacklist, and appeal workflows.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce cooldowns/rate limits, and maintain service reliability.

5. Sharing Rules

We do not sell personal data.

We share data only when needed to run MistBot, including:

  • With service providers listed in this policy to operate bot features.
  • Across MistBot-enabled servers where a feature is intentionally global (for example, global marketplace feed and global blacklist enforcement).
  • When required by law or legal process.
  • When a user explicitly directs sharing through a bot feature.

6. Storage and Security

  • Bot data is primarily stored in Google Sheets secured with service-account based access.
  • Some configuration/state data is stored in bot-controlled files and in-memory caches.
  • Access is restricted to maintainers and authorized systems/services.
  • We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to reduce unauthorized access risk.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

Retention depends on feature purpose:

  • Marketplace listings: generally expire automatically after inactivity (for standard listings, about 6 hours).
  • Ghost/verification trade drafts: short-lived and expire automatically (for example, around 24 hours where enabled).
  • Lobbies: removed after closure/timeout.
  • AI prompts: processed for response generation; not intentionally kept as a permanent user profile dataset by MistBot.
  • Vouches, blacklist, appeals, and moderation records: retained until removed/reset/overturned or no longer needed for the stated purpose.
  • Operational logs/caches: short-lived where possible; some system records may persist for reliability or abuse prevention.

We delete or update data when it is no longer necessary for stated functionality, when legally required, or when a valid user request applies.

8. Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

You can request to access, correct, or delete your MistBot-related data.

How to request:

  • Open a ticket in the support server.
  • Include your Discord user ID and the request type: access, correction, or deletion.

We provide an accessible request path and will handle valid requests within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legal and safety obligations.

Feature-specific controls may also be available in bot commands (for example, deleting listings or submitting blacklist appeals).

9. Discord Developer Terms Alignment

This policy is intended to align with Discord Developer Terms requirements for privacy and security, including:

  • Clear disclosure of collection, use, sharing, and retention.
  • Public availability of a privacy policy.
  • A user-accessible method to request data modification/deletion.
  • Limiting third-party sharing to service providers, legal requirements, or user-directed actions.

If Discord requests data update/deletion related to API data, we will comply as required by applicable terms and law.

10. Children's Privacy

MistBot is intended only for users who meet Discord's minimum age and legal requirements in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the applicable minimum age.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through official MistBot channels, and the date at the top will be updated.
